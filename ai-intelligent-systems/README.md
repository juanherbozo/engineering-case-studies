## Purpose

This section explores how AI capabilities can be evaluated, designed, integrated and governed in real business workflows. It emphasizes **the business problem, technical decisions, limitations and validation**, rather than tools or prompts in isolation.

**Scope:** independent, reproducible learning cases and general technical patterns. This is **not** a collection of customer incidents or copies of confidential project deliverables.

## Case study format

Each published case should describe:

1. **Business context** — the generalized process and objective.
2. **Problem / hypothesis** — what needs to be understood or improved.
3. **Constraints** — versions, capabilities, access, compliance and cost assumptions.
4. **Approach** — experiments, test scenarios and alternatives.
5. **Findings** — observed results, evidence and uncertainty.
6. **Decision / solution** — chosen approach and why.
7. **Validation** — test cases, acceptance criteria and limitations.
8. **Lessons learned** — reusable principles and next steps.

Start with [the English case template](templates/case-study-template.md) or [the Spanish case template](templates/case-study-template.es.md).

## Publication and confidentiality rules

- Reconstruct examples in a personal lab using **synthetic data**. Do not upload customer datasets, screenshots, tickets, messages, proprietary code or internal documents.
- Remove or generalize names, domains, tenant IDs, endpoints, identifiers, timestamps and unusual facts that could re-identify a customer.
- Never include keys, tokens, passwords, connection strings, infrastructure secrets or internal URLs.
- Check employment, customer, contractual and intellectual-property restrictions **before** publishing; anonymization alone may not grant permission.
- State whether a case is **validated**, **illustrative**, or **a hypothesis**. Do not portray imagined test results as observed facts.
- Describe AI output evaluation and human oversight where relevant. Do not provide actual personal or business-sensitive data to public AI services.
- Only publish material you own or are expressly permitted to share.
