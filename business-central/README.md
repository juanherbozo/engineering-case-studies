# Business Central & ERP

[Versión en español](README.es.md) · [Repository home](../README.md)

Independent, generalized case studies about **Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central** and enterprise processes. The emphasis is on functional analysis, observable symptoms, experiments, design decisions and testable outcomes.

| Topic | Folder |
|---|---|
| Finance and accounting | [finance](finance/README.md) |
| Sales and purchasing | [sales-purchasing](sales-purchasing/README.md) |
| Warehouse and inventory | [warehouse-inventory](warehouse-inventory/README.md) |
| Interfaces and integrations | [integrations](integrations/README.md) |
| Upgrades and migrations | [migrations](migrations/README.md) |
| Performance and diagnostics | [performance](performance/README.md) |

**Publication status:** structure only; there are no published customer cases in these folders. Future content must be independently reconstructed with fictitious data, reviewed for confidentiality and intellectual-property obligations, and clearly labeled as tested or illustrative.

Use the [general case study template](../templates/case-study-template.md) before drafting a new case.
